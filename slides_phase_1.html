Problem

A model that predicts humans, not the best move

  • Maia-2 gets one board and two Elo labels, and returns a probability for every legal move.
  • Data: Lichess, June 2024. 10,000 rated blitz and rapid games, 676,579 positions.
  • Black box: we only look at inputs and outputs.
  • It matches the human move half the time. What happens in the other half?

Failures depend on the phase, hardly on the clock

Summary

What we know after Phase I, and what comes next

  • Half of all predictions miss, but most misses are near misses or equally good moves.
  • Real failures concentrate in the opening and on human mistakes.
  • Maia-2 prefers knights and captures more than humans do.
  • GenAI: wider and more careful analysis, fast. Its code had silent bugs, and it produced results faster than we could verify them.
  • Phase II: look inside. Why Nf3 on move one? What does the Elo input change? What happens in confident misses?
